# Rasikh: Abu Dhabi fact check

Checked 2 October 2026. This is a stage and product planning sheet, based mainly on current government, regulator, and service-provider pages. **High** means the linked primary source directly supports the statement. **Medium** means the source supports a narrower statement or practice varies. **Unverified** means the team needs an interview, partner confirmation, or implementation test before presenting it as fact. Requirements can change by visa category, bank, school, free zone, and activity.

## Fix the opening story before the pitch

| Current idea | What we can safely say | Confidence and evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| “Newcomers lose weeks because government visa and ID steps are disconnected.” | The UAE already combines work permits, medical checks, biometrics, residence and ID through its Work Bundle for covered workers. Delays across housing, banks, schools and family arrangements may still exist, but **“weeks lost” needs interview evidence**. | **High** for government integration: [ICP Work Bundle FAQ](https://icp.gov.ae/en/faq/). **Unverified** for the amount of time lost. |
| “You cannot rent until you have an Emirates ID.” | Abu Dhabi’s DARI tenancy workflow accepts a tenant’s Emirates ID **or UID**. We should describe a confusing, provider-specific sequence, not an absolute deadlock. | **High**: [DARI lease registration guide](https://help.dari.ae/en/support/solutions/articles/73000168584-register-tenancy-contract-lessor-). |
| “Rent has to be paid by cheque.” | UAE guidance describes postdated cheques and agreed instalments, but DARI records the agreed **payment method**. We have no current Abu Dhabi statistic for how often cheques are used. | **High** that cheques are an option: [UAE leasing guide](https://u.ae/en/information-and-services/moving-to-the-uae/leasing-a-property-in-the-uae), [DARI guide](https://help.dari.ae/en/support/solutions/articles/73000168584-register-tenancy-contract-lessor-). **Unverified** as a frequency claim. |
| “A bank account is impossible before residence and ID.” | Standard current accounts at two major banks ask for resident documents. A visitor may be eligible for a limited savings account, and a current account does not always include a cheque book. Bank rules and final approval are product-specific. | **High for the named products**: [ADCB current account](https://www.adcb.com/en/personal/accounts/current-savings-account/adcb-current-accounts.aspx), [FAB current account](https://www.bankfab.com/en-ae/personal/accounts/current-accounts/personal-current-account), [ADCB tourist savings](https://www.adcb.com/en/personal/accounts/current-savings-account/instant-tourist-account), [ADCB Universal account](https://www.adcb.com/en/personal/accounts/current-savings-account/universal-account.aspx). |
| “Rasikh is the first joined-up AI setup service.” | TAMM 4.0 already advertises an AI assistant, life-event dashboards and services from public and private partners, including a bank. ADIO advertises investor concierge support and an AI onboarding assistant. Rasikh's proposed employer-led workflow must prove a specific gap these services leave. | **High** for existing services: [TAMM 4.0 announcement](https://www.mediaoffice.abudhabi/en/technology/department-of-government-enablement-launches-tamm-4-during-gitex-global-2025/), [ADIO support](https://www.investwithabudhabi.com/en/setup/support-at-every-step), [ADIO assistant](https://www.investwithabudhabi.com/en/ask). **Unverified** for Rasikh's demand and differentiation. |
| “Only senior executives get relocation help; everyone else is alone.” | Corporate relocation, home and school search, lease help and banking support are already sold by local and global providers. We need interviews to show which hires are underserved and at what price. | **High** for available services: [RelocateMe GCC](https://www.relocatemegcc.com/relocation), [Santa Fe Relocation](https://www.santaferelo.com/corporate-relocation/). **Unverified** for how widely employers buy them. |

## Moving an employee and family

| Step | Verified process and limit for the demo | Confidence and source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Employment residence and Emirates ID | For covered employee routes, the process includes a work entry permit, medical fitness, biometrics, and linked residence and ID applications. Work Bundle connects these government steps. The route and timing vary by category, so do not apply one fixed sequence or deadline to every free-zone employee. Do not show a new physical residence sticker in a passport. | **High**: [ICP Work Bundle FAQ](https://icp.gov.ae/en/faq/), [ICP residence service](https://icp.gov.ae/en/services-details/?serviceid=64afe3c1035448005bd52e64), [ICP on ID replacing the sticker](https://icp.gov.ae/en/media-center/cancellation-of-the-residency-voucher-and-the-consideration-of-the-resident-id-card-as-an-alternative/). |
| Current account | ADCB and FAB list UAE residence/ID and other identity and income evidence for their standard current accounts. ADCB also offers a visitor savings account without a cheque book, and a different current account without a cheque book. Rasikh should track **account eligibility** and **cheque facility** separately. | **High for those banks, not a universal law**: [ADCB current](https://www.adcb.com/en/personal/accounts/current-savings-account/adcb-current-accounts.aspx), [FAB current](https://www.bankfab.com/en-ae/personal/accounts/current-accounts/personal-current-account), [ADCB tourist](https://www.adcb.com/en/personal/accounts/current-savings-account/instant-tourist-account), [ADCB Universal](https://www.adcb.com/en/personal/accounts/current-savings-account/universal-account.aspx). |
| Bank decision | A bank must perform customer due diligence. Employer support may help document income or risk, but it cannot waive checks or promise an account. In the demo, say “packet ready for bank review” and show final approval as the bank's decision. | **High** for bank duties: [Central Bank customer due diligence rule](https://rulebook.centralbank.ae/en/rulebook/62-standard-customer-due-diligence). **Unverified** that a partner bank would accept Rasikh's proposed employer backing. |
| Rent and Tawtheeq | The landlord starts DARI's online tenancy registration, enters tenant details using EID or UID, chooses rent payment method and instalments, and sends the contract for tenant acceptance. Tawtheeq is Abu Dhabi's tenancy registration record. Non-residents can request a DARI profile. | **High**: [DARI registration guide](https://help.dari.ae/en/support/solutions/articles/73000168584-register-tenancy-contract-lessor-), [DARI non-resident profile FAQ](https://help.dari.ae/en/support/solutions/articles/73000623246-can-non-residents-create-profiles-on-dari-). |
| Family residence | The sponsor needs valid residence, sufficient income, adequate housing, proof of relationship and health coverage. ICP states AED 3,000 monthly income with employer housing or AED 4,000 without for the listed family route. Housing evidence is a real dependency, but this ICP service card does **not** say Tawtheeq is required in every case. | **High for this ICP route**: [ICP residence service and family conditions](https://icp.gov.ae/en/services-details/?serviceid=64afe3c1035448005bd52e64). |
| School registration | ADEK's private and charter school policy requires identity and residency documents, but allows a non-UAE transfer student to enrol while an Emirates ID is pending if the parent undertakes to provide it by the end of the term. This is another step that may start before ID delivery. | **High for schools under this policy**: [ADEK Student Administrative Affairs Policy](https://www.adek.gov.ae/-/media/Project/TAMM/ADEK/Policies/School-Policies/Teaching-and-Learning/ADEK_S_Student-Administrative-Affairs-Policy_EN_1_2.pdf). |
| Health insurance | Abu Dhabi requires health coverage for resident workers. Employers are responsible for basic cover for employees and eligible dependants; the regulation limits the employer-covered family category to one spouse and the first three resident children under 18. Coverage is relevant to residence applications. Do not promise that every dependant is covered by an employer. | **High**: [Abu Dhabi Department of Health insurance regulations, Book 2](https://www.doh.gov.ae/-/media/AFAD86A6E3FF447DA030ED194F31CFB6.ashx), [UAE government insurance summary](https://u.ae/en/information-and-services/health-and-fitness/getting-a-health-insurance). |

## Opening a company and moving its team

For a **new employer** sponsoring staff, a company licence and an immigration or establishment file generally precede its employee permit applications. This is a useful sequence for the demo, but people can have other entry and residence routes. [UAE mainland hiring process](https://u.ae/en/information-and-services/business/doing-business-on-the-mainland/recruiting-on-the-mainland-); [ADGM post-registration visa services](https://www.adgm.com/operating-in-adgm/post-registration-services/visa-and-government-services). **Confidence: high for new-company sponsorship.**

| Route or organisation | What is actually offered | Confidence and source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Abu Dhabi mainland | ADDED describes choosing an activity and legal form, trade name, initial approval, required agreements/premises and sector approvals, then an economic licence. Some eligible routes are online or instant. Most mainland activities can now be fully foreign owned; exceptions remain. Do not claim every mainland business needs an Emirati shareholder or a long manual process. | **High**: [ADDED setup steps](https://www.added.gov.ae/en/set-up/establish-your-business), [UAE foreign ownership rules](https://u.ae/en/information-and-services/business/doing-business-on-the-mainland/full-foreign-ownership-of-commercial-companies). |
| ADGM | ADGM's Registration Authority handles incorporation and licensing. The route differs for financial and non-financial activities; regulated financial services require FSRA authorisation. ADGM has its own civil and commercial legal framework based on English common law. An ADGM licence is not blanket permission for all mainland activity. | **High**: [ADGM setup](https://www.adgm.com/setting-up), [FSRA application process](https://www.adgm.com/financial-services-regulatory-authority/fsra-general-application-process), [ADGM authorities](https://www.adgm.com/about/authorities). |
| KEZAD | KEZAD offers **both** a Domestic Economic Zone route and a Free Zone route, with different market access. It is particularly oriented to industrial, trade and logistics businesses. Calling all KEZAD setups “free-zone companies” is wrong. | **High**: [KEZAD jurisdiction comparison](https://www.kezadgroup.com/business-setup/jurisdiction/), [KEZAD free zone](https://www.kezadgroup.com/business-facilities/free-zone/?noredirect=en-US). |
| Masdar City Free Zone | Applicants choose an activity and a free-zone entity or branch route, submit registration material and any needed approvals, then arrange premises and visa services. Package terms and visa quotas vary. | **High**: [Masdar City Free Zone licensing and registration](https://masdarcityfreezone.com/explore/license-and-registration). |
| twofour54 | A specialist media and creative sector free zone with company, branch and freelancer licensing routes. It is not the default route for every industry. | **High**: [twofour54 business setup](https://www.twofour54.com/en/business-setup/). |
| Hub71 | Its Access programme offers selected early-stage startups in-kind support that can include housing, health insurance, licence and visa support, plus investment terms. This is a competitive programme, not an entitlement for any firm relocating to Abu Dhabi. | **High**: [Hub71 Access programme](https://www.hub71.com/program/access-programme). |
| ADIO | ADIO provides investor onboarding, setup and expansion support, incentives, a concierge service and an AI assistant. Support and incentives depend on the company and programme. ADIO does not replace the actual licensing authorities. Rasikh must be clearer than “we give AI setup advice” to stand apart. | **High for advertised services**: [ADIO support](https://www.investwithabudhabi.com/en/setup/support-at-every-step), [ADIO programmes](https://www.investwithabudhabi.com/en/about/programmes), [ADIO AI assistant](https://www.investwithabudhabi.com/en/ask). |

**Tax trap:** Do not say that a free-zone licence means “zero tax” or “no VAT”. The Federal Tax Authority says the 0% corporate tax rate is for qualifying income of a Qualifying Free Zone Person, and VAT treatment in designated zones has conditions. [FTA free-zone corporate tax bulletin](https://tax.gov.ae/Datafolder/Files/Pdf/2024/CT%20Bulletin/Basic%20Tax%20Information%20bulletin-%20Free%20Zone%20Person-English.pdf); [FTA designated-zone VAT guide](https://tax.gov.ae/-/media/Files/FTA/links/vat-designated-zone/Designated-Zones-VAT-Guide.pdf). **Confidence: high.**

## Stage-safe one-sentence problem

“Abu Dhabi already has strong digital services. We are testing whether employers and new hires need a shared place to coordinate the parts of a move that involve their own company, a landlord, a bank and family decisions.” The first sentence is supported by [TAMM 4.0](https://www.mediaoffice.abudhabi/en/technology/department-of-government-enablement-launches-tamm-4-during-gitex-global-2025/) and the [ICP Work Bundle](https://icp.gov.ae/en/faq/); the extra need and product demand **still need interviews**.
