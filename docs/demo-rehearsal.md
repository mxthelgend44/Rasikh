# Demo-day rehearsal checklist

Use the [three-minute script](pitch-script.md) and [nine-slide outline](deck-outline.md). Suggested split: Firas Aleter opens and closes; Mohammad Alhniidi runs the demo and leads technical answers. Swap if you prefer. The checked app commit is a Next.js scaffold with Firebase browser analytics, so any on-screen relocation journey needs to be built or shown as a clearly labelled storyboard.

## The day before

- [ ] Recheck the [fact check](fact-check.md), especially TAMM 4.0, bank approval, UID-based tenancy, and what is simulated.
- [ ] Remove any unverified “weeks lost”, customer, market-size or partner claim from the script and slides.
- [ ] If using an interview quote, record the exact words, context and permission to quote. Otherwise keep the factual opening.
- [ ] Put **Demo mode: simulated government and partner responses** on every relevant screen.
- [ ] Prepare a local, offline click-through or screen recording of the three demo moments: route, hire/consent, partner review.
- [ ] Cache one approved AI explanation for each demo step. Label it **cached response** if used.
- [ ] Use invented sample people and documents. Check that no real passport, ID, address or account data is visible.
- [ ] Run the full pitch three times with a timer; leave five seconds of margin before 3:00.

## Before going on stage

- [ ] Test laptop, power, display cable, resolution, browser zoom, pointer size and slide order on the actual screen.
- [ ] Test WiFi once, then confirm the offline backup opens without WiFi.
- [ ] Open the demo at the first screen; close unrelated tabs and silence notifications.
- [ ] Check every click path and reset all demo data to the starting state.
- [ ] Confirm no mock bank or landlord state says “approved” unless a real partner made that decision.
- [ ] Put one person in charge of the timer and one person in charge of clicks. Agree on the handoff phrase.

## Three-minute run of show

| Time | Lead | Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00-1:02 | Firas Aleter | Factual hook, problem, business hypothesis; advance slides 1-4. |
| 1:02-2:25 | Mohammad Alhniidi | Show route, hire/consent and partner review, in that order. Say out loud that external actions are simulated. |
| 2:25-3:00 | Firas Aleter | Buyer, what needs proving, and the pilot ask. |
| Q&A | Firas, then Mohammad | Firas takes market, buyer and competitor questions. Mohammad takes rule, privacy and integration questions. Both answer what is mocked plainly. |

## Failure switches

- **WiFi fails:** use the local click-through or recorded demo. Say “This is our offline demo recording.” Keep the same three screen moments and timing.
- **Live AI fails or stalls for five seconds:** stop waiting, open the cached explanation and say “This is the cached response for this step.”
- **Prototype crashes:** move to storyboard screenshots in the deck and say “The live prototype has failed; these screens show the intended flow.”
- **Projector or cable fails:** use the printed one-page flow or speak the three demo actions, then return to the ask. Do not claim the audience saw a working integration.

After the pitch, write down judge objections and actual partner interest while fresh. Update the claim sheet before repeating the pitch.
