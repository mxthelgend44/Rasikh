| 1:30-1:58 | **DEMO 2:** hire view. Open document packet and consent controls. Show an AI explanation beside a rule result. | “The hire sees the same journey in plain language. The rules decide eligibility and dependencies. AI explains the result. Before anything is shared, the person chooses which document can go to which party. We are testing an OpenAPPA guard for those permissions.” |
| 1:58-2:25 | **DEMO 3:** landlord and bank views. Show request received, document status, and `pending partner decision`. | “The landlord gets only the housing packet. | 2:25-2:43 | 4. Business and trust model: payer, user, partner decision | “The buyer is the employer. We will measure days from offer to a settled move, waiting time between parties, and manual follow-ups for HR and the hire. Our rules decide the next step, AI explains it, and each provider keeps the final decision.” |
