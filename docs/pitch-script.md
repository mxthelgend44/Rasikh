| Time | Screen | Spoken words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00-0:25 | 1. A simple map: employer, new hire, landlord, bank, school | “Abu Dhabi has already done a lot to make moving here easier. Work Bundle joins the main work and residence steps. TAMM even has an AI assistant. But a company moving a new hire still has its own decisions to track with that person, a landlord, a bank and sometimes a school. We want to test that gap.” |
| 0:25-0:48 | 2. One hire's journey, with different owners on each step | “The order is not the same for everyone. A bank's current account requirements depend on the product. A tenancy can start with a UID before the Emirates ID arrives. A family visa needs housing evidence. HR ends up asking who has which document, who can see it, and what can happen next.” |
| 0:48-1:02 | 3. Rasikh journey overview | “Rasikh is our proposed shared workspace for that move. The employer pays per hire, or for an expansion journey. The newcomer uses it free. That pricing is a hypothesis we are taking to buyers.” |
| 1:02-1:30 | **DEMO 1:** company route selector and hire journey. Click a route, then reveal ordered tasks and owners. | “This is our prototype in demo mode. The company chooses its setup route, then adds a hire. Rasikh gives each person a task and shows why it comes next. Government submissions on this screen are simulated.” |
| 1:30-1:58 | **DEMO 2:** hire view. Open document packet and consent controls. Show an AI explanation beside a rule result. | “The hire sees the same journey in plain language. The rules decide eligibility and dependencies. AI explains the result. Before anything is shared, the person chooses which document can go to which party. We are testing an OpenAPPA guard for those permissions.” |
| 1:58-2:25 | **DEMO 3:** landlord and bank views. Show request received, document status, and `pending partner decision`. | “The landlord gets only the housing packet. The bank gets only the bank packet. An employer can support the application, but the bank still does its own checks. Neither party is shown as having approved anything in this demo.” |
| 2:25-2:43 | 4. Business and trust model: payer, user, partner decision | “The buyer is the employer. The value we need to prove is fewer hours spent chasing, and a calmer first month for the hire. Our approach keeps the route rules separate from the AI explanation, and keeps final decisions with the real provider.” |
| 2:43-3:00 | 5. The ask | “We are looking for three HR teams, one landlord and one bank to test real cases with us. We want to learn where the handoffs actually fail, what employers already pay, and which shared actions partners would accept. Help us turn this prototype into a pilot.” |
