# Evidence for a faster, easier move to Abu Dhabi

**Research checked 2 October 2026.** Rasikh's proposed job is to carry an employer-led move forward: understand the route, prepare and reuse a newcomer's evidence with consent, show each party its next action, and help submit applications where a provider permits it. This dossier asks whether the *process* is hard enough to need that product. It prioritises Abu Dhabi sources, then UAE-wide evidence. Price and rent affordability are outside this question.

## What the evidence says

**The case for easier workflows is real.** Abu Dhabi and UAE authorities have measured or reported large reductions in visits, steps, fields and processing time when they join services. A UAE survey also found that some movers struggled with initial bank-account or utility setup. These are credible reasons to investigate the rest of a move. They do not establish Rasikh's own time savings. [Abu Dhabi Department of Government Enablement](https://www.mediaoffice.abudhabi/en/government-affairs/department-of-government-enablement-scales-up-effortless-customer-experience-strategy/), [UAE Work Bundle](https://www.wam.ae/en/article/13ynvs2-work-bundle-expands-cover-all-emirates), [HSBC/Ipsos survey](https://www.about.hsbc.ae/-/media/uae/en/news-and-media/230301-uae-rated-highest-for-expat-lifestyle-earnings-and-family-stability-pdf.aspx).

**The remaining handoffs are visible.** Official and provider rules show distinct decisions for company sponsorship, residence, a landlord's lease, a bank's account, utilities and family or school enrolment. Some steps depend on earlier documents, while others can start before a physical Emirates ID arrives. The exact path varies by visa route and provider. [ADGM visa services](https://www.adgm.com/operating-in-adgm/visas-and-government-services/corporate-affairs-government-services), [DARI](https://help.dari.ae/en/support/solutions/articles/73000168584-register-tenancy-contract-lessor-), [ADCB](https://www.adcb.com/en/personal/accounts/current-savings-account/adcb-current-accounts.aspx), [ICP](https://icp.gov.ae/en/services-details/?serviceid=64afe3c1035448005bd52e64), [ADEK](https://www.adek.gov.ae/-/media/Project/TAMM/ADEK/Policies/School-Policies/Teaching-and-Learning/ADEK_S_Student-Administrative-Affairs-Policy_EN_1_2.pdf).

**The product claim is still a hypothesis.** We did not find a credible Abu Dhabi study of recent employer-sponsored arrivals that measures the full time to settle, repeated document requests, employer coordination hours, or avoidable days at private-party handoffs. Nor did we find evidence that a Rasikh packet would make a bank or landlord decide sooner. These need timed cases and partner tests. Strong existing services mean the comparison must be with today's Work Bundle and TAMM, not an old all-manual baseline. [Work Bundle](https://icp.gov.ae/en/faq/), [TAMM 4.0](https://www.mediaoffice.abudhabi/en/technology/department-of-government-enablement-launches-tamm-4-during-gitex-global-2025/).

### How to read the proof

| Evidence type | Establishes | Does not establish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reported before-and-after service figures | Process simplification can reduce effort in the named government programmes | Current delay in Rasikh's target journey or savings from Rasikh |
| Survey or first-person account | A respondent experienced or perceived a problem | How often it happens to Abu Dhabi new hires |
| Official or provider workflow | A document, decision, owner or conditional order exists | That every case gets stuck there |
| Pilot with time-stamped cases | Actual wait, rework and effort for the tested route | Population-wide impact without a representative sample |

## 1. The strongest available process evidence

| Finding | Why it matters | Scope and limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Abu Dhabi service redesign.** The Department of Government Enablement said in February 2025 that its Effortless Customer Experience programme had eliminated about **400,000 service-centre visits** and reduced government service steps by **23%**, required fields by **36%**, and customer requests by **64%**. It worked with about 65 entities and companies. [DGE release](https://www.mediaoffice.abudhabi/en/government-affairs/department-of-government-enablement-scales-up-effortless-customer-experience-strategy/) | Direct Abu Dhabi evidence that fewer visits, steps, fields and requests are valued, measurable outcomes. | Government-reported aggregate, without a published matched-case method in the release. It covers many services, not the full journey of a relocating hire or any Rasikh pilot. |
| **Joined employment journey.** At the June 2024 all-emirates Work Bundle launch, the UAE government described a shift from **15 steps and 16 documents to five steps and five documents**, **seven centre visits to two**, and **30 to five working days** for its covered employment and residence journey. [Official launch report](https://www.wam.ae/en/article/13ynvs2-work-bundle-expands-cover-all-emirates) | A close example of the speed gain possible when relevant authorities coordinate. | These are the government's launch figures, not an independently timed Abu Dhabi sample. The bundle already addresses much of the government part of Rasikh's proposed journey. Free-zone routes can differ. [UAE free-zone hiring guidance](https://u.ae/en/information-and-services/business/doing-business-in-free-zones/recruiting-in-free-zones). Do **not** say a current hire normally waits 30 days or that Rasikh saves 25. |
| **Initial setup friction.** In an HSBC/Ipsos survey released in March 2023, **44%** of respondents for the UAE said initial struggles setting up a bank account **or** home utilities unsettled them. The full survey interviewed 7,177 adults across nine host markets. [Release and methodology, pp. 1, 3](https://www.about.hsbc.ae/-/media/uae/en/news-and-media/230301-uae-rated-highest-for-expat-lifestyle-earnings-and-family-stability-pdf.aspx) | The closest published direct report of the kind of task Rasikh might help with. | UAE-wide and older; the UAE subgroup size is absent. Participants included current, prospective and recently returned international movers. The question combines bank and utility setup, so it cannot identify either one's rate or time lost. HSBC sells expat banking. |

**Abu Dhabi-specific qualitative lead, not a statistic:** in a [May 2025 first-person post](https://www.reddit.com/r/abudhabi/comments/1kj5tq1/how_to_validate_if_the_tawtheeq_contract_is/), someone new to Abu Dhabi asked how to check a Tawtheeq contract while their Emirates ID was pending. A [September 2025 post](https://www.reddit.com/r/abudhabi/comments/1nqqbfd/renting_monthly_in_abu_dhabi_is_this_normal/) describes a newcomer in employer-provided temporary housing who was uncertain about renting and Tawtheeq before receiving ID. These unverified accounts illustrate the *questions* people ask. They cannot establish frequency, legal requirements, or a delay caused by those questions.

## 2. Where the current move crosses parties

The route is a conditional workflow, not a universal circle where every step blocks every other step. These sources show what Rasikh could precheck and who must make the real decision.

| Part of the move | Documented owner, input or decision | Important exception |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Opening a company and sponsoring a hire** | A new ADGM entity needs an establishment card and E-Channels account before its employee visa request. Mainland employer guidance also starts with the business licence and establishment file. [ADGM](https://www.adgm.com/operating-in-adgm/visas-and-government-services/corporate-affairs-government-services), [UAE mainland guidance](https://u.ae/en/information-and-services/business/doing-business-on-the-mainland/recruiting-on-the-mainland-) | An already registered employer or a hire with another valid work/residence route has a different path. The Work Bundle does not replace each free zone's rules. |
| **Employment residence and ID** | The [ICP Work Bundle](https://icp.gov.ae/en/faq/) combines covered government stages, including medical, biometrics, residence and Emirates ID. | Treat official status as an input to Rasikh. Do not claim these stages still require five separate platforms for covered users. |
| **Health coverage** | The [ICP residence service](https://icp.gov.ae/en/services-details/?serviceid=64afe3c1035448005bd52e64) lists valid health insurance for the cited family route, and [Abu Dhabi health-insurance regulations](https://www.doh.gov.ae/-/media/AFAD86A6E3FF447DA030ED194F31CFB6.ashx) set employer obligations for eligible employees and dependants. | The responsible party and documents depend on the person's route and coverage. Record this as a case-specific prerequisite, not a universal extra step after the visa. |
| **Resident bank account** | [ADCB's standard current-account checklist](https://www.adcb.com/en/personal/accounts/current-savings-account/adcb-current-accounts.aspx) names a residence visa, Emirates ID and salary certificate. The bank applies its own [customer due-diligence rules](https://rulebook.centralbank.ae/en/rulebook/4411-general-cdd-measures). | Some products permit an earlier start: [ADCB tourist savings](https://www.adcb.com/en/personal/accounts/current-savings-account/instant-tourist-account) and [Wio's visa-number route](https://wio.io/) are examples. Account eligibility, credit and a cheque book are different questions. |
| **Home and registered lease** | The [DARI guide](https://help.dari.ae/en/support/solutions/articles/73000168584-register-tenancy-contract-lessor-) has the landlord enter the tenant using **Emirates ID or UID**, set the agreed payment method, then send the contract for tenant acceptance. One Abu Dhabi operator, [Relaam](https://search.relaam.com/DocumentManager/3/189073/3_189073_254978.pdf), publishes a stricter new-lease document checklist including ID, income and a credit score. | DARI describes the registration process; Relaam's undated checklist is one operator's policy, not Abu Dhabi law. Neither shows that all landlords require a cheque or refuse a newcomer. |
| **Home utilities** | [TAQA Distribution's moving-in guide](https://www.addc.ae/en-US/residential/Pages/About-Moving-in.aspx) says Tawtheeq registration creates the tenant's water and electricity account. | Its [move-in FAQ](https://www.addc.ae/en-us/Residential/pages/ApplicationStatusNotLoggedIn.aspx) says a person awaiting Emirates ID may provide the number within 40 days of a move-in application. The lease matters, but physical ID delivery is not an absolute stop for that route. |
| **Family residence and school** | The [ICP family route](https://icp.gov.ae/en/services-details/?serviceid=64afe3c1035448005bd52e64) asks for sponsor residence, adequate housing, income, kinship and insurance. [ADEK school policy](https://www.adek.gov.ae/-/media/Project/TAMM/ADEK/Policies/School-Policies/Teaching-and-Learning/ADEK_S_Student-Administrative-Affairs-Policy_EN_1_2.pdf) asks for child and parent identity, residence and sometimes transfer records. | ICP's cited route does not say Tawtheeq is mandatory for everyone. ADEK lets certain students transferring from abroad enrol while Emirates ID is pending, with a parent undertaking to provide it by term end. |

**A question to resolve before advising users:** [Abu Dhabi Residents Office's broad housing guide](https://adro.gov.ae/Living-in-Abu-Dhabi/Where-To-Live) says a valid residence visa and Emirates ID are needed to execute a lease, while [DARI's registration guide](https://help.dari.ae/en/support/solutions/articles/73000168584-register-tenancy-contract-lessor-) allows a landlord to enter a tenant using EID **or UID**. Executing a lease and entering an identifier during registration may be different stages. Confirm the practical rule with DARI and the chosen landlord; these pages alone do not establish eligibility before residence or a time loss.

## 3. Does the portable profile idea solve a new problem?

The detailed Rasikh concept adds a consented profile: read documents from abroad, prepare a reliable packet, then help submit it to a landlord, bank or school. There is a practical reason to test this. Similar identity and employment evidence appears across provider checklists: [ADCB asks for residence, ID and salary evidence](https://www.adcb.com/en/personal/accounts/current-savings-account/adcb-current-accounts.aspx); [Relaam's published lease checklist](https://search.relaam.com/DocumentManager/3/189073/3_189073_254978.pdf) asks for passport, ID, salary evidence, bank statements and a credit score; [ADEK](https://www.adek.gov.ae/-/media/Project/TAMM/ADEK/Policies/School-Policies/Teaching-and-Learning/ADEK_S_Student-Administrative-Affairs-Policy_EN_1_2.pdf) asks parents for identity and residence records. **This proves overlapping inputs, not that users currently upload each item multiple times or that one packet would be accepted by every provider.**

The profile must also be described accurately:

- **A parsed document is not a verified document.** AI can extract, translate and compare fields, but authenticity, current validity and source checks require an issuer, official record or accepting partner. A demo should label unconnected checks as simulated and show the underlying source for each claim.
- **Foreign history is not ignored everywhere.** [HSBC UAE](https://www.hsbc.ae/international/credit-cards/faqs/) already uses consented credit history from selected foreign countries in some credit-card applications. This does not mean every bank or landlord accepts it, or that a new current account requires a local credit history.
- **Tenant screening already exists.** In May 2026, [Etihad Credit Bureau announced a live tenant-screening service](https://www.wam.ae/en/article/c09b376-etihad-credit-bureau-launches-tenant-screening) that lets a landlord request a prospective tenant's credit score after UAE PASS consent. Rasikh should not claim to invent the first trusted tenant profile or automatic landlord approval.
- **A more flexible lease is an untested partner decision.** The proposed demo result, “four cheques instead of one because of an AI profile,” has no supporting landlord agreement in these sources. Show a request awaiting landlord review unless a real partner has approved those terms.
- **Consented data sharing is existing infrastructure.** [UAE PASS documentation](https://docs.uaepass.ae/feature-guides/data-sharing-authorization/introduction) describes provider-to-provider sharing with user authorisation. [TDRA also describes a digital wallet](https://tdra.gov.ae/en/media/press-release/2025/uae-digital-achievements) for requesting and sharing official documents. Rasikh would need approved integrations and provider agreements to use such flows in production. [UAE PASS onboarding](https://docs.uaepass.ae/getting-onboarded-with-uae-pass/onboarding-process-for-uae-pass-service-providers/initiation-phase).

**The testable product distinction** is one case that combines route-specific next actions, an employer's evidence, a newcomer's consent and the chosen private providers' actual decisions. We have not shown that this view is missing from TAMM or the providers' own products, or that it saves time. That is the pilot's central question.

### What the evidence suggests building and testing

| Evidence | Proposed Rasikh action | Proof still needed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Work Bundle's once-only information request and shorter covered journey | Prepare reusable, consented inputs for the parts of a move outside that bundle | Whether a bank, landlord or school will accept the packet and whether it reduces repeated work |
| Route and provider rules differ, sometimes allowing a start before physical ID arrives | Show conditional next actions and prepare documents before arrival when allowed | Whether accurate guidance changes real start dates or reduces waiting |
| DARI, TAQA, TAMM and Work Bundle already automate some steps | Read their permitted status and move the shared case forward when another party must act | Whether an approved integration exists and whether it closes a measured coordination gap |

These are product hypotheses derived from the cited workflows, not measured Rasikh effects.

## 4. Evidence against an exaggerated pitch

- **Abu Dhabi performs well on administration.** In InterNations' [2024 city survey](https://www.internations.org/expat-insider/2024/expat-essentials-index-for-cities-40491), Abu Dhabi ranked **second of 53** for Expat Essentials; **80%** of its respondents called getting a visa easy, and **63%** said local bureaucracy was easy. The survey is self-selected, subjective and has an unpublished Abu Dhabi sample size beyond its 50-response inclusion threshold. [Methodology](https://www.internations.org/expat-insider/2024/city-ranking-methodology-40492). An older [2022 city survey](https://www.internations.org/expat-insider/2022/abu-dhabi-40291) found 74% happy with the ease of opening a local bank account. This does not erase individual friction, but it rules out a credible pitch that most people find Abu Dhabi's administration broken.
- **TAMM already acts, guides and connects partners.** [TAMM 4.0](https://www.mediaoffice.abudhabi/en/technology/department-of-government-enablement-launches-tamm-4-during-gitex-global-2025/) advertises an AI assistant that can complete many services, document scanning, life-event spaces and more than **1,100 public and private services** across over 90 partners, including Wio Bank. Its [Family Space](https://www.mediaoffice.abudhabi/en/community/department-of-community-development-launches-family-space-project-at-gitex-global-2025/) includes school-related services. Rasikh cannot claim to be the first AI that acts across Abu Dhabi services.
- **Business setup has already been simplified.** ADDED said it removed **71%** of business setup requirements in 2021 and launched the [Investor Journey](https://www.mediaoffice.abudhabi/en/economy/abu-dhabi-department-of-economic-development-launches-the-investor-journey/) with licensing, premises and a bank-account application path. These are historical programme claims, not proof that every company opens in minutes. They also mean Rasikh's company-setup route must add something specific.
- **Existing relocation firms and employer programmes cover parts of the journey.** [RelocateMe GCC](https://www.relocatemegcc.com/relocation), [Santa Fe](https://www.santaferelo.com/corporate-relocation/) and [Hub71 Access](https://www.hub71.com/program/access-programme) already offer relevant help. No source here establishes that only executives receive help.

## 5. Scale without misusing the numbers

There is activity to investigate: [SCAD counted 4,135,985 Abu Dhabi residents in 2024](https://scad.gov.ae/web/guest/w/abu-dhabi-population-in-2024-grows-7-5-to-reach-4-14m), up **288,400** from the revised 2023 stock, and [Hub71 selected 27 startups headquartered outside the UAE in June 2026](https://www.hub71.com/latest-news/press-release/hub71-selects-27-startups-for-cohort-18-in-first-all-international-intake-after-record-2453-applications), all establishing Abu Dhabi operations. Neither number counts completed work relocations or Rasikh buyers. We did not find a public annual count of people newly arriving in Abu Dhabi for employer-sponsored work. The [market sheet](market-and-competitors.md) holds the calculation limits and alternatives.

## 6. What Rasikh must measure next

The useful outcome is **less effort and less elapsed time for the whole move**, over and above the services people already have. The [interview guide](validation-interviews.md) can identify candidate cases. For a pilot, ask employers for consecutive recent overseas hires, including easy cases. Record the route and the tools each person already used, then compare similar cases against a Rasikh-assisted journey.

| Measure | Record for each case | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **End-to-end elapsed time** | Dates of offer acceptance, arrival, work authorisation, usable account, lease registration, utilities and relevant family or school completion | Prevents a fast visa step from hiding a slow overall move |
| **Waiting at handoffs** | When each prerequisite became available, when the next party received it, when that party acted, and the stated reason for waiting | Separates provider processing from avoidable coordination gaps |
| **User effort** | Forms, visits, messages, document requests, repeated submissions and active minutes for hire and HR | Tests “easier” directly, without translating it into a cost claim |
| **Action and trust** | Which tasks Rasikh actually submitted, which needed user approval, which packets partners accepted or rejected, and what checks were real | Distinguishes an agent from a checklist or a mock screen |

Predefine the success test before running it. A credible result would show fewer touches or shorter handoff waits in comparable cases, with no increase in errors or unsafe sharing. Count easy cases and failed applications too. Until then, “Rasikh makes moves quicker” is a **goal**, not a proven result.

## Stage-safe problem statement

> “Abu Dhabi has shown that connected services can cut steps and visits. Work Bundle has already simplified the work and residence part of a move. But a new hire's full arrival can still require separate decisions from an employer, a landlord, a bank and sometimes a school. We are building Rasikh to carry that shared case forward, prepare the right information with the person's consent, and test whether it makes the whole move faster and easier.”

The first two sentences are supported by [DGE](https://www.mediaoffice.abudhabi/en/government-affairs/department-of-government-enablement-scales-up-effortless-customer-experience-strategy/) and [Work Bundle](https://www.wam.ae/en/article/13ynvs2-work-bundle-expands-cover-all-emirates). The third describes documented [DARI](https://help.dari.ae/en/support/solutions/articles/73000168584-register-tenancy-contract-lessor-), [bank](https://www.adcb.com/en/personal/accounts/current-savings-account/adcb-current-accounts.aspx) and [ADEK](https://www.adek.gov.ae/-/media/Project/TAMM/ADEK/Policies/School-Policies/Teaching-and-Learning/ADEK_S_Student-Administrative-Affairs-Policy_EN_1_2.pdf) handoffs. The last sentence states the product and its unmeasured hypothesis.

### Source limits worth keeping with the deck

- The Work Bundle before-and-after figures and Abu Dhabi service improvements are **government-reported programme figures**. Neither is a controlled Rasikh result or a current estimate of time lost by an individual newcomer.
- The HSBC/Ipsos survey was fielded **5 December 2022 to 23 January 2023**. The UAE-specific sample size was not published in its release. Its 44% result is about **bank setup or utilities**, not a combined Abu Dhabi relocation journey. [Source and methodology](https://www.about.hsbc.ae/-/media/uae/en/news-and-media/230301-uae-rated-highest-for-expat-lifestyle-earnings-and-family-stability-pdf.aspx).
- InterNations' Abu Dhabi city survey was fielded **1–29 February 2024** among 12,543 expats globally, with at least 50 responses required for a city. It measures respondents' satisfaction, not audited process time. [Methodology](https://www.internations.org/expat-insider/2024/city-ranking-methodology-40492).
- Provider checklists describe **named products or operators**, not universal legal rules. A prototype must mark unconnected government, bank, landlord, credit-bureau and school actions as **simulated** until each integration and permission is verified.
