# Market and competitors

Checked 2 October 2026. This sheet separates **published counts** from **assumptions**. A company licence, a work permit, a new resident and a paid relocation are different units. We should never turn one into another without stating the conversion.

## Market estimate

**The requested annual count of people moving to Abu Dhabi for work is not published in the official public sources we checked.** SCAD publishes resident and employed-population stocks. ICP publishes a UAE-wide residence-permit count without the Abu Dhabi, first-time and employment breakdown needed here. Neither number is an annual count of overseas hires. We should say “unknown” on stage rather than turn stock growth into arrivals. Sources: [SCAD Census](https://census.scad.gov.ae/Home/index?lang=en), [ICP statistics portal](https://icp.gov.ae/en/).

### Targets that show direction, not addressable customers

- Abu Dhabi's Tourism Strategy 2030 projects **178,000 new tourism jobs by 2030**. It does not say how many would be overseas hires. [Abu Dhabi Media Office](https://www.mediaoffice.abudhabi/en/tourism/department-of-culture-and-tourism-abu-dhabi-to-deliver-tourism-strategy-2030-to-ensure-emirates-sustainable-growth-as-global-tourism-destination/). **Confidence: high for the target.**
- The Abu Dhabi Industrial Strategy targets **13,600 skilled jobs by 2031**. Its talent plans include Emiratis; it is not a relocation count. [Abu Dhabi Media Office](https://www.mediaoffice.abudhabi/en/economy/khaled-bin-mohamed-bin-zayed-launches-abu-dhabi-industrial-strategy-to-strengthen-the-emirates-position-as-the-regions-most-competitive-industrial-hub/). **Confidence: high for the target.**
- The Abu Dhabi Government Digital Strategy 2025-2027 targets an AI-native government and commits AED 13 billion. This supports the timing for digital workflows **and** makes government a formidable competitor. [Abu Dhabi Media Office](https://www.mediaoffice.abudhabi/en/government-affairs/abu-dhabi-government-launches-digital-strategy-2025-2027/). **Confidence: high for the announced plan.**

### A usable bottom-up calculation

For a pilot, count actual overseas hires from each interviewed employer's last 12 months. Then calculate:

`annual paid journeys = sum(overseas hires at target employers × share with a relevant move × share adopting Rasikh)`

`annual revenue = annual paid journeys × agreed price per hire + paid expansion journeys × agreed price per expansion`

All terms after “overseas hires” are **assumptions** until employers and partners validate them. An example for planning only: **10 employers × 20 overseas hires each × 50% relevant moves × 25% adoption = 25 paid journeys a year.** Every number in that example is hypothetical, not Abu Dhabi market data. For an emirate-wide estimate, the missing input is annual Abu Dhabi hires relocated from outside the UAE, split by employer type. Request that figure from ICP/MOHRE or build a sample from employer records before putting a market-size claim on stage.

**Competitive read:** Rasikh cannot claim to be the first end-to-end relocation coordinator or the first AI agent spanning Abu Dhabi services. Its proposed wedge is employer-led, lower-friction coordination of a specific hire with private parties, consent and shared status. Price, adoption and partner access remain unproven.
