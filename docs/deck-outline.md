# Nine-slide deck outline

One image or diagram per slide. Keep on-screen words sparse. Put source links in speaker notes, not in tiny slide text. Any prototype screen must say **Demo mode** if government, bank or landlord events are simulated.

| # | Title | One thing to remember | Visual |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | One move, many decision-makers | A hire's move spans an employer, the hire, a landlord, a bank and sometimes a school. | Five simple people/organisation icons connected to one hire. Use a real, consented interview quote later; no invented quote now. |
| 2 | The sequence changes by case | The hard part to test is keeping owners and dependencies clear, including exceptions. | A short journey with parallel and conditional branches, not a fake circular deadlock. |
| 3 | Joining steps saves time | The UAE reports that Work Bundle shortened its covered journey from 30 to five working days. The full move still involves other parties, whose handoff time has not been measured here. | One before-and-after card for the government's [2024 Work Bundle launch figures](https://www.wam.ae/en/article/13ynvs2-work-bundle-expands-cover-all-emirates); a separate, clearly marked question at employer and private-provider handoffs. Do not present 30 days as today's baseline. |
| 4 | Rasikh's proposed shared journey | Each party sees its next action and only the information it is allowed to see. | One central journey with four role views: newcomer, employer, landlord, bank. |
| 5 | Demo: company to hire | A setup route can generate owned tasks for an expanding company and its hire. | Live prototype or clearly labelled storyboard of route selector and task list. |
| 6 | Demo: hire and consent | The rule decides the next step; AI explains it; the person controls sharing. | Hire screen with rule result, explanation and explicit per-party consent. |
| 7 | Demo: partner review | Employer support helps a packet reach a partner, while the landlord or bank decides. | Two requests showing `pending review`, never a fake approval. |
| 8 | Prove a quicker, easier move | Proposed payer: employer per hire or expansion. The outcome to test is less elapsed time and effort across the full move. | Three pilot measures: days from offer to settled milestones, waiting time at handoffs, and manual touches for hire and HR. Mark them “to measure”; leave pricing for buyer interviews. |
| 9 | Help us pilot it | Ask for three HR teams, one landlord and one bank to test real cases. | Large numeric ask and a small contact/QR code. |

No market-size number or customer quote goes on the deck until the team can trace it to a source or a consented interview. The [problem evidence](problem-evidence.md), [market sheet](market-and-competitors.md) and [fact check](fact-check.md) hold the supporting detail.
