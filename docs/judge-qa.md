# Fifteen hard judge questions

These are short answers to rehearse. State the prototype's actual status on stage. At the checked GitHub commit, the app is a Next.js scaffold with Firebase browser analytics and no relocation workflow or partner integration.

| # | Question | Honest answer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Why will TAMM not just do this? | It might. [TAMM 4.0](https://www.mediaoffice.abudhabi/en/technology/department-of-government-enablement-launches-tamm-4-during-gitex-global-2025/) already has AI, life-event journeys and private partners. Our test is whether employers need a shared workspace for their own hires and off-platform decisions. We would link to official services where access is authorised. |
| 2 | What does your TAMM integration actually do? | In the demo, TAMM responses are simulated. We have no verified production API access or partnership. A real integration requires permission, a documented interface and end-to-end tests. |
| 3 | How is this different from RelocateMe or Santa Fe? | [RelocateMe](https://www.relocatemegcc.com/relocation) and [Santa Fe](https://www.santaferelo.com/corporate-relocation/) already coordinate broad moves. Our possible difference is a repeatable employer-led, shared-status workflow for more ordinary hires. We have not proved its price or demand. |
| 4 | Why would an employer pay per hire? | HR may value time saved and a smoother arrival, but that is a buying hypothesis. The [interview script](validation-interviews.md) asks what they spent and who approved it. We will price only after real cases. |
| 5 | What is your market size? | There is no verified Abu Dhabi annual count of inbound work movers in our sources. [SCAD](https://census.scad.gov.ae/Home/index?lang=en) reports population and employment stocks; those are not annual arrivals. We show an assumption-led model in the [market sheet](market-and-competitors.md), and will not present stock growth as customers. |
| 6 | Can employer backing make a bank open an account early? | No promise. A bank must do its own [customer due diligence](https://rulebook.centralbank.ae/en/rulebook/62-standard-customer-due-diligence). The employer can prepare verified information. The bank decides eligibility and whether cheques are available. |
| 7 | Why would a landlord accept an employer's support? | We do not know yet. We need a landlord to say what evidence or guarantee would change a real decision, and under what terms. The demo shows a request pending review, not approval. |
| 8 | How do you verify documents? | For the prototype, document fields and status are sample data. Production verification would need issuer checks or authorised partner review, with a visible audit trail. We will not label an uploaded image “verified” by AI alone. |
| 9 | What if your sequence is wrong for a visa or free-zone route? | The route rules must be versioned by authority, activity and permit category, with a source and review date. Exceptions go to a human. AI explains a rule; it does not silently make the rule. The [fact check](fact-check.md) records known exceptions. |
| 10 | What if the AI gives harmful advice? | We would ground answers in reviewed rules, show the source and date, and block an action when the route is uncertain. A human or authority makes legal, banking and tenancy decisions. The live product still needs this testing. |
| 11 | Does OpenAPPA make personal data compliant? | No. [OpenAPPA](https://github.com/archestra-ai/OpenAPPA) is a preview information-flow engine. It may enforce a configured sharing policy, but Rasikh still needs implementation tests, access controls, retention rules and a review against [UAE data-protection obligations](https://u.ae/en/about-the-uae/digital-uae/data/data-protection-laws.). |
| 12 | What is real, and what is mocked? | The checked GitHub commits contain a Next.js scaffold and Firebase browser analytics. The research and pitch materials are real. Relocation screens and government, landlord and bank events are proposed demo flows. We will label each simulation on screen and update this answer as code is built. |
| 13 | Must a company be formed before anyone can move? | A new company normally needs a licence and establishment file before **it** sponsors employees, per [mainland guidance](https://u.ae/en/information-and-services/business/doing-business-on-the-mainland/recruiting-on-the-mainland-). Other visa routes and employers exist. Our workflow must ask which route applies. |
| 14 | Are free zones tax free, and why choose one? | No blanket tax-free claim. Free-zone corporate tax treatment depends on qualifying status and income; VAT has conditions. Choice depends on activity, regulation, premises and market access. See the [FTA guidance](https://tax.gov.ae/Datafolder/Files/Pdf/2024/CT%20Bulletin/Basic%20Tax%20Information%20bulletin-%20Free%20Zone%20Person-English.pdf). |
| 15 | What would make you stop or change direction? | If HR teams show little measurable pain or no budget, or if landlords and banks will not act on a shared packet, we would narrow to a tool for an existing relocation or PRO provider. The pilot must measure time spent, current spend and actual partner decisions. |
